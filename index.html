How it works

↑

Drop your file

Drag and drop any photo or video onto the upload area, or click to browse your files. No account needed.

◈

Colors stay true

Each character takes the exact color from that spot in your image — a blue sky stays blue, a green tree stays green.

⊞

Adjust the detail

Slide between fine and coarse to get more detail or a bolder, more graphic look. Pick from three character styles too.

▶

Video plays live

Upload a video and it plays back as living ASCII art in real time. Works with MP4, WebM, MOV, and GIF.
